# Hyzer July-Window Influencer Outreach Angle

## Core Angle

The July decision window is the event hook. Hyzer is the launch system around it: public positioning, creator education, operator intake, partner routing, and claim-safe demand capture.

The pitch is not “we are the regulated provider.” The pitch is: the market is about to pay attention, and serious people should have their story, audience path, and operator funnel ready before attention spikes.

## Who To Target

### Creator Partners
- Trusted explainers in regulated-market explainers, ecommerce, creator-led commerce, wellness-adjacent audiences, and business commentary.
- Best fit: people whose audience already asks “what does this mean for me?” after regulatory or market news.
- Offer: early brief, clean talking points, audience-safe guardrails, and a routed early-access link.

### Operator Pilots
- Founders, clinics, ecommerce operators, affiliate teams, agencies, and community owners preparing to move quickly around the category.
- Best fit: teams with distribution, an offer thesis, and a willingness to keep regulated operations with qualified partners.
- Offer: funnel positioning, qualification flow, objection map, and launch coordination.

### Partner Tracks
- Legal/compliance reviewers, qualified service partners, operations-adjacent teams, analytics/affiliate teams, and media-buying partners.
- Best fit: partners who can state their role precisely and support operators without pushing unsupported public claims.
- Offer: partner framing, handoff boundaries, and launch-readiness coordination.

## Message Pillars

1. **Timing:** July creates urgency, attention, and confusion.
2. **Preparation:** The winners will already have distribution and intake live when the news hits.
3. **Responsibility:** Hyzer does not pretend to be the regulated provider; public claims stay clean.
4. **Conversion:** Attention should route into early access, operator pilots, and partner conversations.
5. **Trust:** Creators should educate first and sell second.

## Do-Not-Say List

- Do not say Hyzer provides care, regulated services, regulated logistics, regulated partner networks, or payment rails unless those claims are separately verified and approved.
- Do not promise a July outcome.
- Do not imply creators can make unsupported product or regulated-service claims.
- Do not present the unfinished static site as a finished regulated product.
- Do not compare directly against the reference site in public copy.
